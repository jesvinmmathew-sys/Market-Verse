# Legal wording reconciliation

Resolved in repository source on 5 October 2026. These changes update legal copy only; authentication, data processing, storage behavior, infrastructure, and the MIT license are unchanged. Deployment is a separate step and has not been performed.

| Earlier inconsistency | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Website prohibited copying and commercial redistribution despite the MIT license. | Legal modal now explains MIT permissions, notice requirements, and third-party rights. Landing footer identifies the project author without an unverified corporate suffix. |
| Signals were described universally as live and statistical. | Financial notice now discloses delayed, cached, simulated, seeded, and fallback data, heuristic scores, simplified calculations, and timestamp limits. |
| Gemini privacy copy promised enterprise handling and no training for every API request. | Privacy panel describes server-mediated processing and applicable provider conditions without asserting a deployment plan or blanket training/human-review exclusion. Links to [Google's terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). |
| Legal headings and privacy text implied certified compliance or institutional-grade protection. | Neutral legal/privacy headings and no-certification wording replace those assurances. |
| An undated risk statistic and unsupported capability list appeared in the risk notice. | Replaced with a direct warning about substantial trading losses, especially leveraged/derivatives trading, and no guaranteed outcomes. |
| Cookie text claimed telemetry collection and storage that could not be disabled. | Describes actual browser storage, consequences of clearing/blocking it, and a saved analytics preference with no connected collector. Does not claim control over third-party services. |

The no-real-funds/orders, educational/simulation, no-advice, no-advisory-relationship, no-buy/sell-recommendation, no-guaranteed-returns/predictions, and user-responsibility disclosures remain explicit. Liability wording preserves rights that cannot lawfully be excluded, consistent with TERMS.md.

The README and TERMS.md no longer carry unresolved-conflict warnings. This note records source-level reconciliation, not legal enforceability, regulatory approval, a security audit, or a claim that the public deployment has already changed.
